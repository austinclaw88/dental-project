# Verification Discovery Interview Script

**Purpose:** Convert anecdote ("my friend said Cleer was pricey and slow") into decision-grade evidence before writing more code. Run with 8–12 practice owners / office managers. 20 minutes each. The interviewer's job is to listen for *behavior and dollars*, not opinions — what they DO and PAY today beats what they SAY they'd do.

**Who to interview:** your friend first (Cleer user — competitive teardown), then study-club peers, OpenDental community contacts, and at least 2 office managers (they're the champion buyer; owners often don't know the real workflow).

---

## The 10 questions

**1. Walk me through how tomorrow's schedule gets verified today. Who does it, when, and how long does it take?**
*Listening for:* staff-hours/day (the cost baseline), portal-vs-phone mix, whether it happens at all for recall patients. Red flag: "we only verify new patients" (smaller problem than assumed). Green flag: a named person losing 2+ hrs/day.

**2. What does verification cost you today — outsourcer fees, or the loaded cost of whoever does it?**
*Listening for:* an actual number. If they use a service (incl. Cleer/Zuub/etc.): monthly cost, per-verification cost. This calibrates pricing directly.

**3. Have you tried a verification tool or service? Which one, and are you still on it? Why / why not?**
*Listening for:* churn reasons — this is the competitive teardown. Push for specifics: turnaround time, payer coverage gaps, accuracy incidents, writeback (did staff re-key results into the PMS?).

**4. When a tool/service gives you a breakdown, does your front desk trust it — or re-verify behind it?**
*Listening for:* the trust problem. If they re-verify, the incumbent's value is ~zero and the provenance-first design matters. Ask what proof would make them stop re-checking.

**5. Which insurance companies are your top 5 by patient volume — and which ones can only be verified by phone?**
*Listening for:* payer concentration (adapter build order) and the phone-only share (voice-agent value). Note every payer named; this becomes the launch coverage matrix.

**6. How many claims a month deny for eligibility/benefits reasons, and what happens to them?**
*Listening for:* quantified downstream cost (write-offs, rework hours). If they don't know — that's itself a finding (the cost is invisible, sell time-saved not denials).

**7. How often does a patient get a bill that surprises them because the estimate was wrong? What does that cost you?**
*Listening for:* the estimate-accuracy pain that justifies the Phase-2 add-on; anger stories = emotional budget.

**8. If a service verified every patient on tomorrow's schedule with a full breakdown, written into OpenDental, done by 7 AM — what would that be worth per month?**
*Listening for:* unanchored WTP first. Then anchor: "$299 / $499 / $799 — where does it stop being obvious?" Note the flinch point, not the polite answer.

**9. What would make you switch from what you do today? What would make you switch BACK after a month?**
*Listening for:* switching triggers (SLA? price? coverage? writeback?) and churn triggers (first accuracy miss? one unsupported payer?). The second question predicts retention better than the first predicts sales.

**10. Would you run a free 60-day pilot in your practice — your real schedule, your payers — starting next quarter?**
*Listening for:* the only answer that counts. A yes with a calendar date is validation; "sounds interesting" is a no. Target: 5 committed pilots from 12 interviews.

## Scoring the batch

| Signal | Green (build) | Red (stop/rethink) |
|---|---|---|
| Staff-hours on verification | ≥2 hrs/day median | <1 hr/day median |
| Current spend exists | ≥half already pay (service or tool) | Most do it ad hoc and feel fine |
| Incumbent churn reasons | Coverage/speed/writeback (fixable by design) | "Didn't need it" (demand problem) |
| WTP flinch point | ≥$499/mo | <$299/mo |
| Committed pilots | ≥5 of 12 | ≤2 of 12 |
| Re-verification behavior | Named as the reason tools failed | Nobody re-checks (trust isn't the wedge) |

**Cleer-user teardown addendum (for the friend):** monthly price paid · per-verification turnaround (request → breakdown in hand) · % of their payers covered · what happens on uncovered payers · does it write into their PMS or produce a PDF · would they switch for same-price-but-7AM-SLA-and-full-coverage · what would they *pay* for that.
